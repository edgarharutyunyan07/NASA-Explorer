Fascinating Facts About Space

A Day on Venus

Takes 243 Earth days to rotate once, but only 225 to orbit the Sun—making a day longer than a year!

Saturn's Rings

Saturn is the only planet less dense than water—it would float in an ocean large enough to hold it.

Neutron Stars

So dense that a teaspoon of neutron star material would weigh about one billion tons on Earth.

Voyager 1

Launched in 1977 and remains humanity's most distant object, traveling through interstellar space.

ISS Sunrises

Astronauts on the ISS experience about 16 sunrises and sunsets every 24 hours as it orbits Earth.

Moonwalk Duration

Apollo 11 astronauts spent just 2.5 hours exploring the lunar surface, yet changed human history.

Exoplanet Count

Over 5,000 confirmed exoplanets discovered, with thousands more waiting to be confirmed.

Light Speed Travel

It would take 70,000 years to reach the nearest star system at current spacecraft speeds.
